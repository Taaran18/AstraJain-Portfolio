1. READ THIS FIRST: WHAT THE NUMBERS ARE AND ARE NOT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PLAIN SUMMARY FOR THE CLIENT
  - No free public tool gave us a monthly search count for any Jaipur astrology phrase
    (for example "astrologer in jaipur"). Every Jaipur and Rajasthan volume in this file
    is therefore marked "n/a" (unavailable). We have NOT guessed numbers for them.
  - Real, published India volumes were found for a handful of big national terms
    ("kundli", "kundali matching", "kundali milan", "numerology", "astrology", "gemstone",
    "birth chart reading" and a few others). These come from Semrush and Ahrefs public
    pages and from Rankhero. They are shown with their source.
  - Semrush and Ahrefs disagree a lot. Example: "kundli" is 450,000 a month on Semrush but
    68,000 on Ahrefs (about 6.6 times apart). Treat every number as "roughly this big",
    not as an exact count.
  - Google Trends, which we normally use to turn those national numbers into local
    estimates, refused every request during this session (HTTP 429 "too many requests",
    then a Google "unusual traffic" page). So this file has NO Trends-based estimates and
    NO measured seasonality. Section 6 explains what we expect and how to check it.
  - Instead, every keyword was checked in Google's own search suggestions (autocomplete,
    India settings). If Google suggests a phrase, real people type it often enough for
    Google to remember it. This gives a RELATIVE ranking (tiers T3 to T5 below), not a
    count.
  - Big national terms like "kundli" or "kundali matching" are mostly people using free
    online calculators (AstroSage, Astrotalk). Few of them will pay a Jaipur astrologer.
    The smaller Jaipur phrases matter more because nearly everyone who types them wants to
    book someone.

TOOLS THAT WORKED (accessed 2026-10-24)
  a) Semrush public "Website Traffic" pages (semrush.com/website/<domain>/overview/). Each
     shows that site's top 5 organic keywords with an India monthly volume, data month
     September 2026. Used: astrotalk.com, astrosage.com, astroyogi.com, ganeshaspeaks.com,
     clickastro.com, prokerala.com, vinaybajrangi.com, astroindia.com, astrodisha.com.
     Search-result snippets of astrologicalmusings.com (April 2026) and brajeshgautam.com
     (July 2026) pages were also seen.
  b) Ahrefs public website pages (ahrefs.com/websites/<domain>, which redirect to
     ahrefstop.com/websites/<domain>). Top 5 India keywords with volume, no data month
     shown. Used: astrotalk.com, astrosage.com, astroyogi.com, ganeshaspeaks.com,
     premastrologer.com.
  c) Rankhero keyword pages (rankhero.com/keywords/<phrase>). Give a global volume and
     sometimes an India split. The page does not name its data source and its tag tool
     mentions Etsy search data, so its figures are treated as LOW confidence. Trend window
     shown: September 2025 to August 2026.
  d) Google autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com, India region, English interface,
     accessed 2026-10-24). 88 seed phrases checked in English, Hinglish and Hindi. Gives
     no numbers; used for tiers, intent and pain points.
  e) Web search and page reads of competitor sites, directories (WeddingWire, JaipurLove,
     Jaipur Unveiled, AddressGuru, RealEstateIndia), review and complaint sites
     (Trustpilot, Voxya, ConsumerComplaints.in) and news reports, for the competitor and
     pain-point sections.

TOOLS THAT DID NOT WORK (accessed 2026-10-24)
  - Google Trends (trends.google.com explore API, both .com and .co.in): HTTP 429 and a
    Google "sorry / unusual traffic" page on every attempt. A retry loop probed it 14
    times, 3 minutes apart (about 40 minutes), with the same result each time. WebFetch of
    the same URL also returned 429.
  - Semrush Keyword Overview (semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?q=...&db=in): page
    shows no volume without a login.
  - Ahrefs Keyword Generator (ahrefs.com/keyword-generator): no figures returned to a
    plain page fetch (needs a browser script / captcha).
  - Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, Moz, Keywords Everywhere: need an account; not
    attempted.
  - Semrush pages for Jaipur astrologer sites (rajeshshrimali.com, rudrakshshrimali.com,
    acharyalavbhushan.com, astrohemant.com, aghordham.com), and for mahavastu.com,
    anytimeastro.com, astrolearn.co: HTTP 404 (too small for Semrush to publish).
  - Ahrefs pages for rajeshshrimali.com, acharyalavbhushan.com, clickastro.com,
    instaastro.com, vinaybajrangi.com: HTTP 404.
  - Rankhero pages for "astrologer near me", "kundli matching" and "kundli": HTTP 404.
    Rankhero "astrology consultation": page exists but says "No Data".
  - Astrotalk and Astroyogi Jaipur pages: content loads by script, only the title could be
    read.
  - Web searches for published Indian astrology keyword-volume tables: none found.

HOW TO READ THE TABLES
  - India/mo = monthly searches in India, as published by the source. "n/a" = no source
    published a number. Where only a close variant has a number, it is shown as "~
    variant" and explained in the notes.
  - Jaipur = Jaipur / Rajasthan monthly searches. No source gave any, so this column is
    "n/a" throughout. It is kept so the column can be filled from Google Keyword Planner
    later (section 10).
  - Src: S = Semrush public page (India, Sep 2026). A = Ahrefs public page (India). R =
    Rankhero (source unnamed). G = Google autocomplete only (no number).
  - Conf (confidence in the India number): Med = published by Semrush or Ahrefs; Low =
    Rankhero, or tools disagree by more than 3 times; n/a = no number.
  - Tier (relative demand, from autocomplete, shown in India/mo when no number exists): T3
    = Google suggests the phrase AND 5 or more longer versions of it (steady demand). T4 =
    Google suggests the phrase with 0 to 4 longer versions, or the phrase was seen only as
    a suggestion for another seed (smaller but real demand). T5 = Google suggests nothing
    for it (lowest; may still get a few searches). Tiers rank phrases against each other
    only; a T3 city phrase is still far smaller than a T3 national phrase.
  - Int (intent): Lc = local, wants to visit or call someone in Jaipur. Tr = wants to book
    or buy, location not stated. In = wants information. Tl = wants a free online
    calculator. Mx = mixed.
  - Rel (relevance to AstroJain): H / M / L.
  - Page: HOME = /, ABOUT = /about, SERV = /services, HORO = /services/horoscope-reading,
    KUND = /services/kundli-matching, PALM = /services/palmistry, NUM =
    /services/numerology, GEM = /services/gemstone-consultation, VASTU =
    /services/vastu-shastra, GALL = /gallery, CONT = /contact. "+FAQ" means answer it in
    an FAQ block on that page. "+blog" means a future article (not in the current site)
    would suit it better.

LIMITATIONS
  - No Jaipur, Rajasthan or seasonal numbers could be measured in this pass.
  - Published volumes cover only the few phrases that big astrology sites happen to rank
    for. Semrush and Ahrefs disagree by up to 6.6 times on the same phrase.
  - Autocomplete reflects popularity but also recency and personalisation; it was
    requested without login and with India settings to reduce that.
  - Competition notes are judgement from who appears in search results, not a tool
    difficulty score.

WHAT THIS TELLS US
  - Kundli matching is the single biggest service demand in India (hundreds of thousands a
    month), but it is mostly free-calculator traffic owned by AstroSage, Astrotalk and
    Prokerala. AstroJain should target the human, paid version: "kundli matching for
    marriage" + Jaipur + Manglik worries.
  - Numerology (110,000) and gemstones (40,500) are large national topics; numerology is a
    realistic second service page to grow.
  - Personal-brand searches for astrologers with an online presence run at a few hundred a
    month (720 and 480 above). That is a realistic 12-month target for "sachin jain
    astrologer" if the client builds Google reviews, YouTube or press.

HOW TO USE THIS IN COPY
  - State the fee and what it includes before the visitor has to call.
  - Say plainly that remedies are optional, low-cost options (mantra, charity, routine)
    are offered first, and gemstones are suggested only after checking the chart.
  - Say birth details and conversations are kept private and not shared.
  - Answer the marriage-delay and Manglik questions on the kundli-matching page in calm,
    non-frightening language; mention that Manglik dosha often has cancellations.
  - Do not promise results ("guaranteed", "100% solution"). It is exactly what distrustful
    searchers are trying to avoid, and it is a legal and reputational risk.
  - Confirm every factual claim with the client before publishing (years of practice,
    client count, whether site visits for Vastu are offered, whether gemstones are sold).
