ASTROJAIN - COLOUR PSYCHOLOGY AND HOW IT IS APPLIED ON THE WEBSITE
=========================================================================
Date        : 2026-10-24 (all sources below accessed on this date)
Audience    : Families and professionals in Jaipur and across India looking for an
              astrologer they can trust with marriage, career, money and home decisions.
Goal        : Build trust fast, signal spiritual depth without looking "mystical and scary",
              and make the one action that matters (Book Consultation) impossible to miss.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. READ THIS FIRST: HOW STRONG IS THE EVIDENCE?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Colour psychology is often oversold online. Most blog posts repeat claims without
    citing studies, and several pages found during research were low-quality or copied.
  - The most cited peer-reviewed work is Labrecque and Milne (2012), "Exciting red and
    competent blue: the importance of color in marketing", Journal of the Academy of
    Marketing Science 40(5), 711-727. Secondary summaries report that blue is linked to
    competence, red to excitement, white to sincerity, and black and purple to
    sophistication, and that saturation and brightness change these impressions. The full
    text was not available here, so exact effect sizes are not quoted.
  - Every summary agrees on one caveat: colours have no fixed meaning. Meaning comes from
    culture, context and personal experience. For an Indian audience, Hindu colour
    symbolism matters as much as Western marketing research.
  - The only firm UX principle used here is contrast (the von Restorff or isolation effect):
    one item that looks different from everything around it is noticed and remembered.
    It is about contrast, not about one "magic" button colour.
  - So the palette below is a reasoned design choice backed by the best available
    evidence, not a guarantee of higher conversions. The website's own analytics and
    A/B tests are the real proof.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PALETTE AND WHY EACH COLOUR IS THERE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

MIDNIGHT INDIGO / NAVY  (dark theme background #070A1C, light theme text ink #151634)
  Psychology : Blue is the most consistently linked colour to competence, trust and
               stability (Labrecque and Milne 2012; widely used by banks and payments).
               Deep, muted blue reads as calm authority rather than excitement.
  Fit        : It is also the colour of the night sky, the natural setting of astrology.
  Used for   : Dark theme surfaces, all body text in light theme, the top info strip, the
               booking call-to-action panel, the logo mark.

GOLD  (#D4A843 fill, #8C630E as text on ivory, #E4BD64 as text on midnight)
  Psychology : Yellow and gold are associated in Hindu culture with learning, wisdom,
               auspiciousness and prosperity (Vasant Panchami, turmeric rituals). Gold,
               used sparingly, signals value and premium quality.
  Fit        : Jupiter (Guru), the planet of wisdom and fortune, is traditionally linked
               with yellow and gold.
  Used for   : The ONE filled primary button style (Book Consultation, Send Request), the
               brand name, key numbers. Because only primary actions are filled gold, they
               stand apart from every other element (isolation effect).
  Rule       : Never use a filled gold background for anything that is not the primary
               action on that screen.

SAFFRON  (#B84A06 on ivory, #FF9F43 on midnight)
  Psychology : Saffron (kesariya, bhagwa) is the most sacred colour in Hinduism, linked to
               Agni (fire), purification, light and the pursuit of enlightenment. It is warm
               and attention-drawing without the alarm of red.
  Used for   : Section eyebrow labels with the diamond ornament, numbered menu items,
               small sacred accents. It tells the visitor "this is rooted in Indian
               tradition" at a glance.
  Rule       : Accent only. Small text and icons, never large areas or buttons.

VIOLET  (#5838BE on ivory, #AA91FF on midnight)
  Psychology : Purple is linked in secondary research to sophistication, spirituality and
               intuition, and is associated with the crown chakra. Sources disagree on its
               emotional tone, so it is kept as a supporting colour.
  Used for   : Icons for client concerns, keyboard focus rings, the starfield in light theme.

IVORY / PARCHMENT  (light theme background #FBF8F1, tint #F5F0E4)
  Psychology : White is linked to sincerity and purity. A warm off-white feels calmer and
               more personal than clinical pure white, and reduces glare for long reading.
  Fit        : Echoes paper kundlis and patrikas.
  Used for   : Light theme page background and cards.

GREEN  (WhatsApp #25D366, success #15803D / #4ADE80)
  Psychology : Universally read as "go" and "done". WhatsApp green is instantly recognised
               by Indian users.
  Used for   : Only the WhatsApp buttons and success states (request received, ticks).

RED  (#BE2626 / #F87171)
  Psychology : Alarm and urgency. Using red loosely would make an astrology site feel
               fear-based, which is exactly the pain point clients complain about.
  Used for   : Only form errors and the destructive "Clear Form" confirmation.

3. ACCESSIBILITY CHECKS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Body text, links and labels meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1) in both themes. Text gold
    and saffron use darker shades on ivory and lighter shades on midnight for this reason
    (saffron: about 4.9:1 on ivory, about 9.7:1 on midnight).
  - Dark navy text on the gold button is used in both themes; it passes AA comfortably.
  - Colour is never the only signal: errors also have an icon and text, the active menu
    item also has a marker, selected options also have a filled radio.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO KEEP IT CONSISTENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - All colours live as tokens in app/globals.css (:root for light, .dark for dark) and
    are exposed to Tailwind in tailwind.config.ts (paper, card, tint, line, fg, gold,
    saffron, violet, success, danger, brand, night, cream).
  - Add new UI with these tokens, never raw hex values, so both themes stay correct.
  - If a future A/B test is run, test the CTA wording and placement before the colour;
    contrast matters more than hue.
